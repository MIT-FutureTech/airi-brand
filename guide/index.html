MIT AI Risk Brand Guide

The look of the MIT AI Risk Initiative, including the AI Risk Repository and the AI Risk Index. One logo, one icon, one typeface, one red, and fixed meanings for colour in data. Version 2.0.0.

7 hard rules. Everything else is a default.

  1. Use the logo files as they are: never redrawn, recoloured, stretched or merged with another mark. There is one AIRI logo for all products. It must look the same everywhere.
  2. AIRI red is #A32035, taken from the theme, the template or the token. Never approximate it, and never add a red from outside the kit. Near-reds are the most common drift.
  3. Figtree for all text, with the fallback stack Figtree, Arial, sans-serif. Code may use the monospace stack. One typeface ties the products together.
  4. Text meets WCAG AA contrast: 4.5 to 1, or 3 to 1 for large text. People must be able to read it.
  5. Colour never carries meaning alone. A status colour comes with a word or an icon. Some readers cannot tell colours apart.
  6. In data, red never means good, and a red scale never shows a neutral amount. Everywhere else in AIRI work red means risk.
  7. Nothing flashes more than three times a second, and every video has captions. Flashing can trigger seizures, and many people watch without sound.

The rest of this guide shows defaults: a starting point, and the answer when nobody has said otherwise. People can stray from a default on purpose, and a little drift is fine. Claude follows the defaults unless a person asks for something different. Where the kit says nothing, Claude reuses the closest pattern here, or picks the plainest option, and says so.

3 · Font & colour

Colour in one breath

  1. One red: #A32035. Take it from the theme, the template or the token.
  2. Two text colours: ink #1A1A1A and muted #666666. White on red or dark.
  3. In data, red is the subject, the finding or more risk. Grey #898A8D is context. Ink #1A1A1A is a comparison. Blue shades a neutral amount.
  4. A figure uses a red scale or a red highlight, not both.

Figtree, the only typeface

Figtree is used for everything, including the wordmark. It is free from Google Fonts. Set text left-aligned and in sentence case. Slides, documents and figures use regular and bold; the web may also use semibold.

  • Colour a grouping only when the colour says something. Bars that compare groups are one colour; the names and the lengths do the work. Pick one out in red, or give related groups one colour.
  • Use the colours where each group needs its own mark: a diagram or schematic with a few named parts, a stacked bar, one line per group.
  • More than eight groups: grey with names on the chart, and colour only the one or two the chart is about.
  • Subgroups take lighter and darker steps of their parent's colour. Two groupings at once: colour one, put the other on the axis.
  • Keep a group's colour the same within one paper or product.

Charts made in Google Sheets, Slides, PowerPoint or Excel take the theme accents in order: red, grey, ink, then category colours 1 to 3. That suits a chart of one to three series. For named groups, set each series to its slot colour by hand.

5 · Open questions

Not yet settled. Each has a default to use until it is. Open a GitHub issue to weigh in.

newDark mode

Dark values are defined in brand.css and the artifact template. They pass contrast checks but have not been proven in shipped work or on a projector. Treat them as provisional.

newPrint colour

AIRI red has not been matched to a print ink. One proof is needed before the next printed report.

openImage and illustration style

The kit has no rules for imagery yet. Report covers use one white line texture each on red. Whether each product should have its own motif is undecided.

openTemplate files for Slides, Docs, PowerPoint and Word

A document template and a social card ship in templates/files/. There is no generated slide deck: the first attempt, in November 2026, was not good enough. Decks start from a recent AIRI deck or a Claude slide artifact. Whether long reports get a template or a set of good example reports is still open.

More detail: skill/BRAND.md is the full specification. CONTRIBUTING.md explains how the files fit together.

Questions or proposed changes: open an issue on GitHub. Retired logos are in legacy/logos/.
